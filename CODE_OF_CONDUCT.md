# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive engineering discussion and contributions from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:
- communicate respectfully
- critique technical ideas rather than people
- provide reproducible evidence where practical
- respect licensing, privacy and confidentiality
- avoid harassment, discrimination and abusive behavior

Unacceptable behavior may be reported through the repository's support or security channels. Maintainers may moderate or remove content that violates these expectations.
