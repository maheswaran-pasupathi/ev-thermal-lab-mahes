# Roadmap

The roadmap communicates intended product direction; timing may change as models are verified.

## v0.2 — Drive-cycle energy
- time-domain speed traces
- acceleration/inertial force
- SOC trajectory
- regenerative braking
- WLTP-compatible cycle input

## v0.3 — Route and environment
- real-route coordinates
- elevation and smoothed grade
- weather time histories
- wind direction resolved against vehicle heading
- precipitation / wet-road treatment

## v0.4 — Battery performance
- LFP/NMC presets
- OCV and resistance dependence
- SOH / usable-energy effects
- battery current and heat generation

## v0.5 — Thermal management
- lumped battery thermal model
- coolant heat-removal estimate
- battery cooling demand
- hot/cold ambient scenarios

## v0.6 — HVAC and preconditioning
- cabin thermal load
- cooling/heating COP
- battery preconditioning energy

## v1.0 — Validated integrated release
- documented public validation cases
- route + weather + battery + thermal integration
- scenario comparison and reporting
